# Support

## Getting started

1. Install **Couchpad Receiver** on your Mac and open it. Click **Turn On**.
2. When macOS asks, allow Couchpad under **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility**. This lets it move the pointer and type for you.
3. Click **Pair iPhone…** on the Mac. A QR code appears.
4. On your iPhone, open **Couchpad** and tap **Scan Pairing Code** (or just point the iPhone's Camera at the code).

From then on your iPhone connects to your Mac automatically when both are on the same Wi-Fi.

## Common questions

**My iPhone doesn't see my Mac.**
Make sure Couchpad on the Mac says it's on, and both devices are on the same Wi-Fi. On the iPhone, check **Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Couchpad** is on.

**It connects, but the pointer doesn't move.**
On the Mac, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility** and make sure **Couchpad** is turned on. If it already is, turn it off and on again.

**The trackpad stopped working and says my Mac is locked.**
For your security, Couchpad ignores the trackpad and keyboard while the Mac is locked, so nobody can type at your login screen from a phone. Unlock the Mac, or turn this off in Couchpad on the Mac (Security).

**Can someone else on my Wi-Fi control my Mac?**
No. Only iPhones you pair yourself – by scanning the code shown on your Mac's screen – can connect, and everything is end-to-end encrypted. For extra safety you can turn on **Ask before each connection** on the Mac and **Require Face ID** on the iPhone.

**How do I remove an iPhone or a Mac?**
On the Mac: **Paired iPhones → trash icon**. On the iPhone: **Settings → Paired Macs**, swipe left.

**Screen Mirroring says it can't find the menu.**
On the Mac, open **System Settings → Control Center → Screen Mirroring** and choose **Always Show in Menu Bar**.

**The pointer feels laggy.**
Use the same 5 GHz Wi-Fi for both devices and keep **Direct connection** off.
