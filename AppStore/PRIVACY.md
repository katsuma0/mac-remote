# Couchpad Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Couchpad is an iPhone app and a Mac app that let you control your own Mac from your iPhone.

## The short version

**Couchpad does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you do in Couchpad is sent to us or to anyone else.

## How it works

- The iPhone app talks **directly to your Mac over your local network** (Wi-Fi, or an optional direct Wi-Fi link). It never connects to the internet or to any server.
- Everything sent between your iPhone and your Mac (pointer movement, clicks, what you type, volume and other commands) is **end-to-end encrypted** with keys that only your two devices have.
- Only iPhones that you pair yourself, by scanning a code shown on your Mac's screen, can connect to your Mac.

## Data stored on your devices

- **On your iPhone:** the names of the Macs you paired with and a secret pairing key for each one (kept in the iPhone Keychain, on that device only, never backed up or synced), plus your settings such as pointer speed.
- **On your Mac:** the names of the iPhones paired with it, their pairing keys and the Mac's settings. These are kept in the app's own private storage.

This data never leaves your devices. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Permissions

- **Local Network (iPhone and Mac):** to find your Mac and connect to it.
- **Camera (iPhone, optional):** only to scan the pairing code on your Mac. Images are processed on the device and never stored or sent anywhere.
- **Accessibility (Mac):** to move the pointer, click, type, and open the Screen Mirroring menu when you ask from your iPhone.

## Children

Couchpad doesn't collect any data from anyone, including children.
